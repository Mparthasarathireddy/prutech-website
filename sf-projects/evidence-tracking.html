Evidence Management & Tracking Solution

Complete evidence lifecycle management for labs, victim services and law enforcement.

Built on Salesforce Service Cloud

End-to-end tracking and management of sensitive evidence throughout its lifecycle, with complete chain of custody from collection through long-term retention. Role-based access serves facility staff, warehouse personnel, administrators and victims. An anonymous victim portal with PIN-based access lets individuals track evidence status without login credentials, while barcode/QR integration and automated notifications ensure efficiency and victim-centered care.
