Affordable Housing Placement Solution

Transform how your agency manages affordable housing from application to placement.

Built on Salesforce Public Sector Solutions

The platform connects developers, government agencies and residents in one ecosystem. Developers submit properties and manage applicant pools through a dedicated portal. Agencies maintain full compliance oversight with automated approval workflows and real-time visibility. Residents use a public portal to browse listings, submit applications and track status online.
